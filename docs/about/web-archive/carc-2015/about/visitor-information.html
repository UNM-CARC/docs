Visitor Information & Parking

The Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC) is located at 1601 Central Avenue NE, Albuquerque, NM 87106, in the former Galles Building.

The Center is located on the north side of Central Avenue, one block west of University Blvd.  The parking lot is to the west of the building on Ash Street.  If you park in this lot, you will need to obtain a one day visitor parking permit at the front desk of the Center.  Faculty, staff, students, and residents at the Center can obtain authorization for a longterm "P" parking pass with the purchase of a regular campus parking sticker from UNM Parking and Transportation Services.

Driving Directions

The Center is located on the north side of Central Avenue, one block west of University Blvd.  

From Albuquerque Sunport Rental Cars:
When you leave the rental car facility, you will be on University Blvd.  Unfortunately, University Blvd. doesn't go through to Central Avenue.  If you head south on University Blvd., it will turn to the right and end at Yale Blvd.  Turn left onto Yale and continue with directions "From Albuquerque Sunport Terminal".

From Albuquerque Sunport Terminal:
Go North on Yale out of the airport.  Turn left (west) onto Gibson. Turn right (north) onto University.  Go three miles north to Central Ave. Turn left (west) onto Central Ave.  Continue one block to Ash St.  Turn right onto Ash St. and take an immediate right into the CARC parking lot.

From Northbound I-25:
Take the Lead/Coal exit. Turn right (east) on Coal.  Turn left (north) on University Blvd.  Turn left (west) onto Central Ave.  Continue one block to Ash St.  Turn right onto Ash St. and take an immediate right into the CARC parking lot.

From Southbound I-25:
Drive south through the Big-I; take the Dr. Martin Luther King exit.  Turn left onto Dr. Martin Luther King.  Turn right (South) onto Ash St.  Take Ash St. 2 1/2 blocks, then turn left into the Center parking lot.

From both east and westbound I-40:
Take the exit for I-25 South to Socorro and Las Cruces and follow directions above.
