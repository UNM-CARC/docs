Associated Faculty & Visiting Researchers

Associated Faculty of the Center are researchers who are collaborating with the Center through participation in supercomputer or storage consortia, grant collaborations, or who serve as facutly mentors of students participating in the Computational Science and Engineering Certificate Program.  For information on affiliating with the Center as an Associated Faculty Member, please contact director@carc.unm.edu.
