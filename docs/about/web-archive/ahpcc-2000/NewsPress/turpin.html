AHPCC and Access Grid Host Turpin

A staffer for the office of US Senator Pete Domenici visited the AHPCC on Thursday August 10. Elizabeth Turpin took a tour of the AHPCC building learned of the different computer systems housed at the center. She was also shown the progress of the research activities that run through the AHPCC. She was then given a demonstration on the Alliance Access Grid. Turpin interacted with researchers on the grid from different nodes.

The Argonne National Laboratory, Kansas University, the MHPCC, and several other sites (also run by UNM) all helped run the demonstration over the grid.
