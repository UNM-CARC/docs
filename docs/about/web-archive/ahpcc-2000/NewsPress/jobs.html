Available Positions

The Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center is accepting applications for systems personnel. This is a tremendous opportunity to push the boundaries of computing. One of the focuses of the AHPCC is Linux superclusters.

Senior Applications Support Analyst
Under indirect supervision, oversees, leads, and coordinates the development, installation, maintenance, and/or modification of scientific or engineering application programs on parallel computer systems. Provides guidance and direction in establishing the functions required for the successful development and implementation of tools required by users/customers. Maintains a broad knowledge of state-of-the-art technology, software, and/or systems relative to high performance computing.

Systems Analyst III
Under limited supervision, provides complex system-level support of multi-user operating systems hardware & software tools including installation, configuration, maintenance & support of these systems. Requires extensive knowledge of systems analysis. Designs & plans systems & serves as a team leader. Will do the following: Operate, maintain & administer Linux cluster systems for parallel computing; Set-up & maintain system services including mail, NIS, NFS, DNS, automounter & backups; Install, configure & test computing & networking hardware; Monitor performance of systems, including tuning; Perform diagnoses & on-call support for computer/network systems; Install software, write scripts & programs for system & network management; Develop & implement security procedures. Will work with vendors & other supercomputing sites. May recommend or purchase systems.

Manager of Systems & Programming
Manager of the high performance computing system and systems group at HPCERC, to include storage, networks and visualization. Responsible to: plan, implement and manage a computer services group to provide support to HPC users and users of other Center services; provide systems support for the Center and for UNM researchers in computing sciences and technology research and development; plan and implement computing systems improvements, upgrades and state-of-the art accessions; provide technical assessments and evaluations of computing systems and computing system administration as required; perform as spokesperson for Center computing capabilities.

MAUI/SR TECHNOLOGY MANAGER Deadline for Application 9/1/00
Under limited supervision, provides direction, coordination and oversight for major segments of the MHPCC Operations Directorate. The Senior Technology Manager will direct multiple, organizational elements and will be responsible for all aspects of the high performance computing infrastructure. Specific areas of responsibility will include high performance computer systems, networks, production operations, user services and other technology issues as appropriate. The incumbent will coordinate the development and execution of projects and initiatives, provide technical program direction, and participate in strategic planning for the center. Additional duties include the development and establishment of departmental standards and procedures, independent development of project plans, budgetary requirements, and staff and resource assessments. Works closely with other members of the MHPCC executive staff to provide a comprehensive support program for a wide range of DOD, commercial, academic and international customers.
