Vista Azul Mailing Lists

vista-azul-announce

vista-azul-announce provides a communication path between UNM, IBM, and other project participants. It will be used primarily for announcements of major miletones or for discussions related to the project as a whole. Postings to vista-azul-announce are limited to the list owner. Subscription requests to this list must also be approved by the owner. However, the policy is pretty lenient!

Subscribe Unsubscribe Help

vista-azul-technical

vista-azul-technical is used to facilitate technical discussions and status reporting internal to the Vista Azul project.

Subscribe Unsubscribe Help

vista-azul-users

vista-azul-users has two purposes: to provide a mechanism for system administrators to communicate with users of the Vista Azul cluster, and to provide a channel among user to make suggestions or to talk with each other about Vista Azul.

Subscribe Unsubscribe Help

Of course, you are welcome to subscribe to any or all of these lists! However, please refrain from cross-posting messages.
