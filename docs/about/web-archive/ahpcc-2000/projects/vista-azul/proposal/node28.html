   The above configuration plus


8 additional RedHawk Switch Adapters for the Netfinity nodes

Other equipment to connect to RoadRunner to IBM cluster will be acquired by AHPCC. This includes a Myrinet/Gbit Ethernet link to a Myrinet Switch (expected 2Q00), 3D Graphics cards (GTX2000P) for the Netfinity nodes, a Gigabit switch supporting Jumbo frames, 8 programmable network interface cards, and possible additions of Myrinet adapters to Netfinity (Linux) or SMP (AIX) nodes.
