Acceptable Use

By accepting an UNM-Alliance Roadrunner Supercluster account, I agree that:

  1. I will be responsible for all use of this computer account.
  2. I will use the computer account for High Performance Computing purposes and not for commercial purposes.
  3. I will not use the computer account to engage in any form of illegal software copying or other copyright infringement.
  4. I will not attempt to access accounts, files or information belonging to other users without their knowledge and consent.
  5. I understand the monitoring of user activity on the Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center machines may occur.


FAILURE TO COMPLY WITH THESE RULES WILL RESULT IN REMOVAL OF THIS ACCOUNT, AND POSSIBLE DISCIPLINARY OR LEGAL ACTION

Security

Users are provided an initial Default Password in their new user packet. You should change your password the first time you login and periodically thereafter. If it is necessary for you to request a password change from us (for example, when you have forgotten your current password), your password will be set to this default. It is important that you keep this form in a secure place for your reference. If you do not have this form available, you will be required to wait until a duplicate can be mailed to you via U.S. mail. AHPCC does NOT provide passwords by phone, fax, or electronic mail.
