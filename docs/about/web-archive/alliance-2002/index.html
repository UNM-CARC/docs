Welcome...
The High Performance Computing, Education and Research Center (HPCERC) is a strategic center of the University of New Mexico.

HPCERC recognizes the different possibilities within high performance computing (HPC). To better accomplish UNM's mission of service, education, and research, HPCERC has formed divisions. The Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center (AHPCC) serves the multidisciplinary aspects of UNM. The Maui Scientific Research Center (MSRC) serves as a hub for space research.


HPC & Collaboration...
The term "high performance computing" (HPC) encompasses many disciplines. Artists, educators, biologists, chemists, engineers, and many others collaborate through HPC. Tour the HPCERC web site to learn more about HPC.


Consulting...
HPCERC serves as a single point of contact for expertise in HPC, networking, educational initiatives, technical support, and many other areas.

Users and technology partners often take advantage of the AHPCC´s innovations in Linux Superclusters. We use these resources to make advancements at UNM, Alliance, and industry.

 

 The High Performance Computing, Education, and Research Center is a core University resource created to support multidisciplinary and multi-institutional collaborations by providing research, consulting, education, and computing resources in high performance computing at the university, state, and national levels.
