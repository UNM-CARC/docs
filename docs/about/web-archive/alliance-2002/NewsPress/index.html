AHPCC has World's Fastest Ranked Linux Cluster
AHPCC made the top 500 supercomputer list at number 80 - 4th among US academic institutions. Overall C-plant at SNL was at 84 and MHPCC clocked in at 57 with a SP to be delivered in December (that is allowed). Our AHPCC team did the hard work of actually benchmarking LosLobos. It appears to be the highest rated Linux cluster on the list.		 November 6, 2000
Campanulacae Runs in Record Time
Using the largest open, production Linux supercluster, LosLobos, researchers at the AHPCC have achieved a nearly one-million-fold speedup in solving the computationally-hard phylogeny reconstruction problem for the family of twelve Bluebell species (scientific name: Campanulacae) from the flowers' chloroplast gene order data.

MPICH Test Results for 8/10/00 Chris Maestas made available the results of running the performance tests from the mpich distribution. They are available in gnuplot and postscript format. The tests were run on the original TURING cluster, Black Bear (BB), Los Lobos (LL), Road Runner (RR), and Vista Azul (AZUL). I still have yet to gather data from the new workshop cluster (TRUCHAS). I usually ran the tests on 8 nodes, with the exception of LL and RR, where I ran on 1 node, 2 processors, in order to compare the change in latency. Please scroll down after you load each dir to view software version information and information on the naming scheme of the files. August 10, 2000
