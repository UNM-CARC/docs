Dear users of CARC's research computing facilities,

After a long stretch of quiet and reliable systems operation, the
Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC) will be resuming a
periodic maintenance downtime schedule.  We anticipate quarterly
shutdowns.  These will not typically exceed a day in length, but
some may be longer, depending on the work to be done.  Such work
might include maintenance on or installation of supercomputing
and storage systems, supporting IT infrastructure, and electrical,
cooling, safety, and security equipment associated with our various
machine room spaces, labs, etc.


Our next maintenance period will be two days long:

  Wednesday, June 16  through  Thursday, June 17.


The schedule:

 (1) Wednesday, June 16, 7:00 AM - 8:00 AM:

     During this hour, there will be intermittent network
     connectivity (including the wireless system) within
     the Galles building and to outside networks as we
     perform network-related maintenance.

 (2) Wednesday, June 16, 8:00 AM:

     Full network connectivity into the Center will resume.

     At this time we will take down the following research
     systems and services (see below for more detail):

       (A) The ERD and Alliance subnets' NFS file services;

       (B) The email interface to the Trouble Ticket system;

       (C) The Nano and Poblano supercomputer systems;

       (D) Any individual or group research web pages and/or code
           archives (cvs, svn) that are hosted on the ERD NFS file
           system;

       (E) Any research desktop using the ERD NFS file system;
           e.g., some research student desktops and some systems
           in the Visualization Laboratory.  (Please check with
           CARC staff if you are uncertain about your particular
           desktop.)

       (F) All desktop systems in the Workshop room.

 (3) Thursday, June 17, 5:00 PM:

       All CARC computing services will be restored by this time.


Some details:  Please note...

(1) We will be performing full (as opposed to incremental)
    backups just prior to this maintenance period.

(2) The Ristra and Pequena supercomputing systems (including
    access to Encanto through Pequena) will not be taken down
    at any time during this two-day maintenance period.  These
    systems will continue to run, though their head nodes may
    be inaccessible intermittently (and ssh connection to
    Encanto may need to be re-established) during the network
    maintenance hour.

(3) During much of this maintenance period, the CARC Trouble
    Ticket System's email interface will not be available,
    due to NFS file system maintenance.

    Trouble reports can still be submitted in one of two ways:

     (A) You may use a web interface to the Ticket System:

           https://www.hpc.unm.edu/irs/ticket

         Note that the web equivalent to the email interface's
         automated response is a second web page that appears
         once you have pressed 'submit'.  This second page
         contains an acknowledgment of the submission, along
         with the Ticket Number, which may be used in future
         correspondences with the CARC regarding your ticket.

     (B) You may call the Center's main telephone number:

           505-277-8249

     (Please note that these trouble reporting procedures may
      be changed for future maintenance periods.)

(4) Jobs submitted to Nano's Torque ("PBS") batch system prior
    to the shutdown will start only if their wallclock requests
    indicate that they will complete before 8:00 AM on Wednesday.
    Users may expect that any jobs that are submitted to Nano
    just prior to the maintenance and that do not start will be
    retained and will start at the end of the maintenance period.
    That said, it is recommended that Nano users verify after the
    maintenance that no jobs were lost.

(5) Poblano does not use Torque at this time.  Poblano users
    should therefore think carefully about the expected run
    time of any work they start ahead of the maintenance period.
    All running jobs on Poblano will end when it is shut down
    at 8:00 AM on Wednesday, and these jobs can not be retained
    or restarted after the maintenance.  Also, interactive jobs
    may be killed by the intermittent network outages between
    7 and 8 AM on Wednesday.

(6) The core services of the Visualization Laboratory, the
    SRBCB, CREATE, the OSG, the ARTS Lab and the NMCAC
    Visualization Gateway will not be affected, other than
    during the network maintenance period, when accessibility
    will be intermittent and ssh connections may be lost.


If you have any questions or comments about this maintenance
period, please feel free to contact the Center.

Sincerely,

Tim Thomas
CARC Operations Director
