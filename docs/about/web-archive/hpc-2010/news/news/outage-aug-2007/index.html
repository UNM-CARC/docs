HPC Scheduled Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance for August 15, 2007

What Announcement Machine Maintenance
When 2007-08-15 08:00 AM 2007-08-15 05:00 PM 2007-08-15
from 08:00 am to 05:00 pm
Contact Name HPC Systems Group
Contact Email help@hpc.unm.edu
Contact Phone 505-277-8249
Add event to calendar vCal
iCal 
The primary fileservers at the Center for High Performance Computing
at University of New Mexico will be taken off line for maintenance
Wednesday, Aug 15, 2007 at 8:00 AM. The following machines will be
affected:

    Mailer (mail will be queued, but not delivered)
    all RCDE boxes
    all Trucha boxes
    Poblano
    Pequin
    Nano
    Rio Rancho
    Los Lobos

    The web server will not be serving user's home pages at this time.

We expect the fileservers to be back up by 5:00 PM Wednesday.


Please contact the HPC@UNM helpdesk at help@hpc.unm.edu if you have
questions or concerns.

Thank you
HPC System Staff
