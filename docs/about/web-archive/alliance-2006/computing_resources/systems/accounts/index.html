About Projects, Accounts, and Logins

Projects

HPC@UNM structures your usage of our resources according to Projects. A Project is the primary unit for tracking users and usage. A single Project may include many users and can use many different resources. On the other hand, a single user may be active in several different Projects.

Basically, a Project indicates a specific research or administration activity that spans time and consumes computational cycles or other resources. Every Project has a point-of-contact, called the Principal Investigator. The Principal Investigator is responsible for the activities carried out under the project.

  • Apply for a Project

Accounts

An Account corresponds to a single user (like you), using a single resource (like Los Lobos), under the care of a single Project. Accounts are specific to Projects, to people, to machines, and to your login information.

  • You can't have an Account without a Project.
  • Accounts are specific to login name and groups. You can't have an Account without login information.
  • Accounts are specific to machines. So you have a login on LosLobos, and the same login on RoadRunner, you have two Accounts, but only one login. The two Accounts can specify the same project, or different Projects. It all depends on what you are doing.
  • You can have two Accounts on the same machine (with the same login) if the Projects differ or the groups differ.
  • All usage is billed to Accounts, and from there to Projects.
  • Users can view their usage, grouped by Account. Principal investigators can also view their Project's usage.

When you apply for an Account, the system will inquire about the Project and PI.

  • Apply for an Account using an existing Project.
  • Apply for an additional account to access a different resource.
