RESEARCH ASSISTANT

Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering

(Experimental Parallel Algorithmics)

Research Assistant positions are available in the High Performance Computing Lab of the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering, University of New Mexico. The assistant will work in collaboration with Professor David Bader at the University of New Mexico to investigate parallel algorithms and programming methodologies for clusters of symmetric multiprocessors. A successful candidate will have both a strong theoretical and practical computer engineering background in the areas of parallel algorithms, architectures, and programming languages.

Each research assistant will receive a stipend of $16,000 per year, plus paid tuition.

The University of New Mexico maintains a rich, collaborative environment for high performance computing with the Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center (affiliated with the National Computational Science Alliance (NCSA), Sandia National Laboratories, Los Alamos National Laboratories, and the Maui High Performance Computing Center.

Informal inquiries may be addressed to Dr. David Bader (dbader@eece.unm.edu).

Applicants should send a detailed CV and transcripts, together with the names and phone numbers of three references, to Dr. David Bader, Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering, EECE Building, University of New Mexico, Albuquerque, NM 87131.

The University of New Mexico, which is both a Carnegie I Research University and a minority-serving institution, is an equal opportunity/affirmative action employer and educator.
