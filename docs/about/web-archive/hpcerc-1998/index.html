Welcome to the Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center (AHPCC)

The AHPCC is a center of the High Performance Computing Education & Research Center,
a strategic center of the University of New Mexico.

The mission of AHPCC is:

"To provide a focus for research and education in advanced high performance computing
and interdisciplinary applications, and state-of-the-art communications."
