Dr. Tom Caudell is developing Flatland, a virtual reality environment. The intent is to provide an environment where data may be visually interpreted. Its uses include, but are not limited to, doctors examining MRI's in 3D, electrical engineers detecting extra electrical signals from a fly's eye, and visualization of programs. The views above represent the output a molecular dynamics simulation and a visualization of the running parallel program. Views complements of Dr. Paul Alsing, Kim Edlund, and Ken Summers.
Welcome...

The Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center (AHPCC) provides services in educational and computational sciences.  It is associated with the University of New Mexico (UNM).

Users and technology partners often take advantage of the AHPCC´s innovations in Linux Superclusters. We use these resources to make advancements at UNM, Alliance, and industry.

In the News...

Staff from Senator Domenici visits UNM Telehealth/Cybermedicine Initiatives
Ryan Gleason, staff member of Senator Domenici, visits UNM to learn about telehealth activities. Project TOUCH, Flatland, AccessGrid, and Virtual Collaborative Clinic are working to provide healthcare education in innovative ways.

Performance Tuning in Microprocessor-based Systems
This half-day workshop demonstrated several techniques for improving the single-processor performance of applications on clusters of IA32- and IA64-based systems, such as those in service at OSC, NCSA, and AHPCC. A more complete description is available on the General Announcement.

Tribal Virtual Network
TVN is a consortium of New Mexican Tribes helping each other cross the digital divide. Catch up on their progress or register for the January 28 workshop.

Linux Clusters Institute
LCI teaches the applications and systems of Linux clusters. Leaders in the Linux clusters, IBM, HPCERC, and NCSA, have partnered together to promote the use of scalable systems. Register for the next workshop at: http://www.linuxclustersinstitute.org/registration.html

CS471
UNM, UMT, and UAK at Fairbanks are the first to team-teach an accredited course over the AccessGrid. Many lessons are being learned for future AG courses.

SCGlobal
The AccessGrid's version of SC2001 is SCGlobal. UNM presented three presentations. Read up on their status.

Bioinformatics Online
Those interested in bioinformatics, there is a free bioinformatics course online that may be a one-time trial. Several universities around the world are contributing to this course. The URL is: http://s-star.org.

Education, Outreach and Training
Advances in wireless technologies are benefiting collaborative education.

Project TOUCH is Advancing
Project TOUCH will demonstrate the feasibility of employing advanced computing methods to enhance education in an innovative problem-based learning (PBL) format currently being used in the medical school curriculum, applying specific clinical case models, and deploying to remote sites/workstations.
