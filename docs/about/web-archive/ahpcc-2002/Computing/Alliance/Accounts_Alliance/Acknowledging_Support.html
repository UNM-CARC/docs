Guidelines for Acknowledging Support

An acknowledgement of UNM-Alliance support must appear in publications of any material, whether copyrighted or not, based on or developed with resources of the University of New Mexico and the Alliance.

 

Suggested Wording
    We would like to thank the University of New Mexico for access to their Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center. This work utilized the UNM-Alliance Supercluster.

 

Submitting Copies

Copies of technical reports and publications using AHPCC resources should be sent to AHPCC with the grant number listed with your login information.
