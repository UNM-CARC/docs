    Section 5. Computer Use Agreement

    By signing this agreement, I agree to abide by the Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center's policy governing ethical computer use. To see this policy before obtaining a computer account, click here.

    In particular, by accepting an Albuquerque High Performance Comoputing Center computer account, I agree that:

    1. I will be responsible for all use of this computer account.
    2. I will use the computer account for High Performance Computing purposes and not for commercial purposes.
    3. I will not use the computer account to engage in any form of illegal software copying or other copyright infringement.
    4. I will not attempt to access accounts, files or information belonging to other users without their knowledge and consent.
    5. I understand the monitoring of user activity on the Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center machines may occur.

    FAILURE TO COMPLY WITH THESE RULES WILL RESULT IN REMOVAL OF THIS ACCOUNT, AND POSSIBLE DISCIPLINARY OR LEGAL ACTION

    Section 6. Publication and acknowledgement

    Users are encouraged to publish results of efforts performed, in professional journals or deliver papers at scientific forums. All authors are encouraged to submit two reprints of each published paper to the AHPCC Director of User Services.

    DoD Sponsored Efforts

      Prior to release for DoD sponsored efforts, users shall clear publication with the applicable Public Affairs Office.

      Authors shall include the following statement in all manuscripts, news releases, advertising copy, published professional papers, or presentations submitted for publication.

    All Other Efforts

      All other efforts include independent Center research efforts and gratuitous Center services.

      For all other efforts, authors shall submit one copy of each manuscript or abstract accepted for publication to the Albuquerque High Performance Computing Center Director for User Services.

      Authors shall include the following statement in all manuscripts, news releases, advertising copy, published professional papers, or presentations they submit for publication.

      "Research sponsored in part by the Phillips Laboratory, Air Force Materiel Command, USAF, under cooperative agreement number F29601-93-2-0001.
