Graduating AHPCC Research Assistants
Eleven students at the AHPCC graduated December 15, 2000. The AHPCC congratulates them on their superior achievements. Their contributions gave tremendous innovation to their fields of science. We are honored to have them as AHPCC alumni.

Dr. Paul Bennett, Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics
Chun-Yu Chan, BS in Computer Science
 Lijun Deng, MS in Computer Science
Dr. Karen Haines, Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering
Rajanikanth Jayaseelan, MS in Civil Engineering
Jian Li, MS in Computer Science
Jeff Shuckra, MA in Instructional Technologies
Dr. David Tyler, Ph.D. in Optical Physics
Vinila Yarlagadda, MS in Computer Science

Cheng Zhou, MS in Chemistry
