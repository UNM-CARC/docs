 Lodging and Driving Directions

Driving Directions:

 Hotels near the University that give discounts for UNM:

From Albuquerque Sunport:
Head N. on Yale out of the airport
Turn left (west) onto Gibson
Turn right (north) onto University
Go three miles north to Central Ave.
Turn left (west) onto Central Ave. 1 block to Ash St.
Turn right onto Ash St. and take an immediate right into our parking lot.
Walk 1/2 block up Central Ave. to our main entrance located on the east side of the building.

From Northbound I-25:
Take the Lead/Coal exit
Drive 4 blocks north beside the highway to Central
Right on Central 2 blocks to Spruce 1 block to Copper
Right on Copper go 3 blocks to Ash,
right on Ash
left into our Parking lot
Walk 1/2 block up Central to our main entrance located on the east side of the building.

From Southbound I-25:
South through the Big-I take Dr. Martin Luther King exit
Left onto MLK
Right east to Ash
Right on Ash 2 1/2 blocks turn left into our parking lot
Walk 1/2 block up central to our main entrance located on the east side of the building.

From both east and westbound I-40:
Take the exit for I-25 South to Socorro and Las Cruces and follow directions above.
