# About CARC

Mission, facilities, partner cyberinfrastructure, and this documentation project.

* [Mission and vision](mission.md) - CARC's vision and mission: leading and growing the computational research community at UNM.
* [Facilities description](facilities.md) - Boilerplate facilities description for grant proposals: clusters, storage, networking, and the data center.
* [History of CARC computing](history.md) - Historical record of the supercomputers in the Galles Building machine room, 1994 to today: machines, funding, processing power, costs, directors, and the PIs who contributed hardware.
* [Web archive of the old center websites](web-archive.md) - Browsable copies of the HPCERC, AHPCC, Alliance Supercluster, HPC@UNM and early CARC websites, 1998 to 2016, rebuilt from Internet Archive captures with working local links and images.
* [Partner cyberinfrastructure](partners.md) - National and regional platforms CARC users can reach: ACCESS-CI, Jetstream2, CyVerse, and MESA.
* [Contributing to these docs](contributing.md) - How to edit pages, the OKF frontmatter contract, verifying migrated content, and building the site locally.
* [For AI agents](ai-agents.md) - How agents and harnesses should consume this documentation: llms.txt, per-page Markdown with OKF frontmatter, raw source on GitHub, trust signals, and what to do if you cannot fetch this site.

* [Documentation update log](../log.md) - Chronological history of changes to this documentation bundle.
